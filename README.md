#Power BI Portfolio
##Exploratory analysis of UK Road Traffic accidnet (2005-2015)
Tools: Power BI - Power Query - DAX
Dataset: UKSTATS19 Road Safety Data
Records: ~2 Million Accidents
###Project Overview
•	Cleaned and transformed multi-year accident data using Power Query 
•	Decoded categorical variables (severity, weather, lighting, road conditions) 
•	Created DAX measures for accident counts and severity percentages 
•	Designed an executive-style dashboard focused on trends and risk factors 
###Key Insights 
•	Serious or fatal accidents account for 15% of all recorded incidents 
•	Overall accident volume declined steadily between 2005 and 2015 
•	Rural accidents are less frequent but more severe than urban accidents 
•	Higher speed limits are associated with increased accident severity 

##Call Centre Operational Analysis & Performance Dashboard 
Tools: Power BI - Power Query - DAX
Dataset: : Call Centre Operations Dataset (Sourced from Kaggle) 
Records: ~1200 Day Index.
###Project Overview
•	Cleaned and transformed raw operational data, including standardising time-based fields for analysis  
•	Developed DAX measures to calculate key KPIs such as abandonment rate, answer rate, and service level  
•	Built an interactive dashboard to analyse call centre performance and identify operational trends  
•	Used data visualisation techniques to explore relationships between call volume, wait time, and service outcomes 
 
###Key Insights 
•	Increased wait times were strongly linked to higher call abandonment, highlighting a key driver of customer drop-off. 
•	Higher call volumes correlated with reduced service levels, suggesting capacity constraints during peak periods.  
•	Performance improved in recent periods, with lower abandonment rates and higher service levels. 
•	Findings indicate that optimising staffing during peak demand could significantly improve operational performance.
